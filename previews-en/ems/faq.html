Q: How much do you pay per dump?

A: Fair question, but tough to precisely answer. Many factors decide the worth of your logs, some of which include: quantity, consistency, smell, flavor profile, buyer, etc. It's common for two shipments from the same producer to differ in value. At the minimum you can expect to make about 2 poo points per shipment after your probationary period (4-6 weeks) has passed. During your probationary period it is possible that the poo point rewards can be below the minimum.

Q: What is a poo point worth?

A: Great question! Poo points change in value depending on the supply and demand for that quarter. We try our hardest to keep you, the producers, up to date with the foreseeable future that is this business, and providing a quarterly lock on poo point valuation is fair and just in our opinion.

Current: 1 poo point = 140 DOSCoin
