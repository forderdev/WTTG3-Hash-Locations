Your decision has been recorded

Our talent works extremely hard to produce a captivating and gripping performance. They give it their all during a recording to hopefully create an experience that for a moment makes you question its origin.

This exhibition was recorded on location, in an isolated section of sea, far from any who would be within distance to hear it. The pureness of this performance derived from inherent fear; fear that in a body of water with a volume of over 1.35 billion cubic kilometers you are nothing. Being cast into the seemingly infinite surrounding ocean would almost certainly lead to your untimely end. No one would ever find you, nothing would be left of you. The sea and that which naturally inhabits it would remove any indication you were ever there.

Instilling those truths in our subject allowed for what you witnessed above to be created.
